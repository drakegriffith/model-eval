Every dot on this card is a 100% pass rate — all 237 charted runs passed. Across 15 adjacent-rung comparisons and 144 matched pairs, the number of discordant pairs, meaning a task that passed at one tier and failed at the tier next to it, is 0. Sol charges 4.40× more at the top of its ladder than the bottom and buys nothing measurable with it.
Medians are descriptive, not a hypothesis test; the pass claim next to them is the exact McNemar contrast from appendix §3, which needs discordant pairs to have anything to test and never found one. Output tokens only, because Fable’s input-token counts are quarantined as unreliable. The honest limit: effort cannot buy a pass on a task that already passes at the lowest tier, so this establishes the knob is pure cost in this difficulty band, not at every difficulty.
